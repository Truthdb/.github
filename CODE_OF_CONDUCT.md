# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free community for everyone who participates in TruthDB projects.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Give and receive feedback thoughtfully.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Threats of violence, doxxing, or sharing private information.
- Trolling, repeated disruption, or personal attacks.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, chat) and to any other space when an individual is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior they deem inappropriate.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by opening a private Security Advisory in the affected repository (preferred) or by contacting the maintainers through a private channel.

## Attribution

This policy is inspired by common open source community conduct guidelines.
